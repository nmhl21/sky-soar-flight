Sky Soar — Canyon Run: a three.js chase-view flight game. Fly the stunt plane through a glowing ring course over a canyon valley — throttle, pitch and roll on the keyboard, fuel and damage turn each sortie into decisions, and every ring flown is points on the board.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: W/S or Up/Down pitch, A/D or Left/Right roll and turn, Shift throttle up, Ctrl throttle down, Space boost, click start/restart, P pause, R restart, M mute.
